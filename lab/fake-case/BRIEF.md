# Örnek Bank — invented ingestion fixture

This is synthetic test data, not the hackathon case.
The agent collects one credit-card statement debt and speaks Turkish.
Use the call timestamp supplied in `now`. Verify identity before reading debt details.
Record a payment promise only after the customer confirms its amount and date.
Use the service contract for exact endpoint and field names. The account is a single
object; there is no products list. The accompanying Word file contains a sample
conversation, and the workbook has synthetic customers on two sheets.
